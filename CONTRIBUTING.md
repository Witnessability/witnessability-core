# Contributing

## Licensing

Contributions are accepted under the outbound license identified for the affected file or directory. Software defaults to Apache-2.0; conceptual documentation defaults to CC BY 4.0; open schemas may use CC0-1.0. IETF Contributions follow IETF Note Well and applicable BCPs.

## DCO

Contributors use `Signed-off-by` under DCO 1.1. DCO text is not modified.

For AI-assisted or AI-generated contributions, sign-off certifies the contributor's right to submit the material under the outbound license **to the extent applicable rights subsist**. It does not assert exclusive human authorship or copyright where protection may be limited or absent.

## Authorship and AI assistance

Declare one:

- human
- ai_assisted
- ai_generated_human_directed
- mixed
- witness_material_third_party
- record_of_third_party_statement

Disclose the AI system, role, human review/testing/revision, and known rights/confidentiality constraints.

## Third-party witness material

- preserve originator-provided material verbatim where represented as firsthand witness material;
- provide an intake hash;
- document publication/license permission;
- do not silently revise substantive content;
- use a publisher note or new originator version for corrections.

## Confidentiality

Do not submit confidential information to public repositories or IETF channels. Public IETF Contributions are governed by Note Well and carry no confidentiality expectation.
